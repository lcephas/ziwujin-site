Support

Contact ziwujinapp@gmail.com for questions, bug reports, or feedback.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Getting started

ZiWuJin supports iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 16 or later. Apple Pencil is optional; finger writing is supported. Open Settings to choose the curriculum, script, input method, and worksheet layout. You can replay the tutorial from Settings.

Writing and erasing

Use the pencil/eraser control to switch between writing and manual erasing. The trashcan clears all writing on the current sheet. Clearing a sheet removes its saved writing.

Saved writing

Writing is saved locally on your device. ZiWuJin does not provide account-based sync between devices. Removing the app can remove its locally saved writing.

Character guides

The included curriculum contour data is bundled with the app. If a requested character is not available in the bundled data, the app attempts to download its contour data. An internet connection is needed for that fallback.
