A quiet space to practice Chinese characters, one stroke at a time.

Trace clear character guides with your finger or a compatible Apple Pencil. Choose Simplified or Traditional Chinese and a worksheet that fits your practice.

Your practice

Starter characters and HSK 1, 2, and 3 writing sequences. Adjustable guides and grids. Stroke-order animations when you want a closer look.

Your space

No accounts, grading, or subscriptions. Writing stays on your device. Erase a stroke, clear a sheet, or return to a character later.
