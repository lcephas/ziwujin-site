Privacy Policy

ZiWuJin is a Chinese handwriting workbook. No account is required, and your writing is stored locally on your device.

Writing and preferences

The app stores handwriting drawings and practice settings on your device. It does not send your drawings to the developer or provide its own cloud sync. You can remove writing using the app's clearing controls. Removing the app can remove locally stored app data. Device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Network requests

Character contour data is normally read from resources included in the app. If data for a requested character is unavailable locally, the app may request that character's JSON contour file from jsDelivr at cdn.jsdelivr.net. This request includes the requested character in the URL and exposes ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to the service. It does not include your handwriting drawings, support email, or an app account identifier.

jsDelivr handles requests under its own policies. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.

Speech and Apple services

Character pronunciation uses Apple's system speech synthesis. Voice downloads, device backups, App Store purchases, and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are governed by Apple's policies and your system settings.

Analytics and advertising

ZiWuJin contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not sell your personal information.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. That information is used to respond to your request and investigate reported problems, not for marketing. Do not include sensitive information. Contact support to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any legal retention requirements.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no analytics scripts, cookies set by this site, or contact forms. GitHub may process connection information to serve and secure the site under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app does not ask users, including children, to provide personal information or create an account. A parent or guardian should handle support correspondence for a child.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact ziwujinapp@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
